The fixes you've
been waiting for.

Three big fixes that make Quote Reply feel solid from end to end.

Fixed

Jumping to Highlights Now Works

Clicking a quote in the sidebar would sometimes land in the wrong spot. We tracked it down and fixed it — it now jumps exactly where you expect.

New

A Much Cleaner Settings Panel

The extension popup has been completely redesigned — easier to read, easier to use. You can now also turn Quote Reply off entirely without uninstalling.

Fixed

Send & Enter Actually Send Now

Some users found that pressing Enter or clicking Send didn't always go through when a quote was attached. That's now fixed.

New Quota usage bar is now hidden automatically if you're on the free Gemini plan
Fixed Scrollbar no longer shows up in the settings panel when there's nothing to scroll
Improved Your settings and usage stats are never wiped when the extension updates
