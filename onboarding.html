Bookmarks, Table of Contents & Auto Mode bring next-level speed, search, and organization to your Gemini workflow.

New

Response Bookmarks

Save, search, and revisit your favorite Gemini responses anytime. Press Ctrl+Shift+B or open the sidebar drawer to search, copy, or export your saved answers.

New

Table of Contents

Navigate long conversations effortlessly. A dynamic sidebar displays every prompt turn in your chat so you can jump directly to any question with a single tap.

New

Smart Auto Mode

Automatically switches between Gemini models based on task complexity, keeping your chats lightning fast and intelligent without manual dropdown tweaks.

More power features in this release

100% Free Completely Unlocked — Every single feature, bookmark tool, and customization is 100% free with no paywalls or waitlists.
Improved Multi-Quote Stacking — Highlight multiple passages across answers to combine them into one focused follow-up prompt.
Improved Smart Paste — Massive code or text blocks (>4KB) are automatically converted into file attachments so Gemini never lags.
