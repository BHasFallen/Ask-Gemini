What it does with your data.

100% Free, Private & Client-Side. Your chats, prompts, and bookmarks stay on your local device.
01
What it does
Quote Reply for Gemini runs only on gemini.google.com. It adds quote-reply, Smart Paste, Table of Contents, Bookmarks, usage limits and Auto Mode to the Gemini page.
02
Permissions
It asks for storage, unlimited storage and alarms, to keep your settings and bookmarks on your device. It has access to gemini.google.com and to api2.amplitude.com for usage analytics.
03
Analytics
The extension sends usage analytics to Amplitude. Telemetry is strictly limited to aggregated, anonymous UI event counts (such as button clicks) to improve stability. Chat text, prompts, and response contents are NEVER transmitted or logged.
04
Your chats
All quoted snippets, smart paste conversions, bookmarks, and chat history are processed locally in your browser memory and Chrome local storage. Nothing is stored on external servers.
